      About KEKA

      KEKA approaches clothing as a visual language. Precise construction meets softer silhouettes.

      Form
      Silhouette begins with proportion. Structure gives each piece clarity without making it rigid.
      Identity
      Clothing becomes personal when it leaves room for the wearer rather than prescribing who they should be.
      Expression
      Detail is used with intention, allowing character, emotion and individuality to emerge without excess.
