## Platform

web

## Stack

- Live site today: WordPress + WooCommerce + Elementor (Konte theme), Slider Revolution, Mailchimp for WP, currency switcher (USD / EUR), behind SiteGround/Cloudflare bot protection.
- Concept stage: static HTML/CSS/JS prototypes in `concepts/`, no build step.
- Production target undecided. The owner named Shopify's "hangers / interactive wardrobe" storefront trend as a reference; staying on WooCommerce or moving to Shopify is an open decision.

## Users

- People who choose clothes for how they feel and what they say, regardless of gender or age. They find KEKA through runway coverage and Instagram (@keka_fashion), mostly on phones.
- Press, buyers, stylists and collaborators who use the site as the brand's card: collections, lookbooks, runway news.

## Product Purpose

keka.design is the KEKA brand site and its online store in one. It introduces the brand, archives its collections and runway shows, and (once the store opens) sells the pieces directly. Success: a first-time visitor understands KEKA within one screen, browses the wardrobe with pleasure, and reaches the bag without hunting for the shop.

## Positioning

From the brand's own copy: "Fashion as identity. Form as freedom." KEKA treats clothing as a visual language: precise construction and contemporary tailoring meet softer silhouettes, movement between masculine and feminine codes, historical references (the Tudor collection) and contemporary form. "Minimalism is not the absence of detail. It is the decision to leave only what matters." The owner describes the character as quiet luxury and cut, and the line as unisex: the designer says he does not care whether the wearer is a man or a woman.

## Operating Context

- Designer and creative director: Kostiantyn Lieontiev, also founder and producer of London Fashion Day.
- Collections are presented on the runway: TRACE at London Fashion Day Season 7 (1 May 2026), Tudor collection AW25-26 at London Fashion Day Season 5 (April 2025), Harbin Fashion Week (January 2026), debut men's collection at London Fashion Day (April 2024); earlier shows at Odessa and Lviv Fashion Weeks.
- Lookbooks on the current site: SS24, SS18, Cruise.
- Garments produced with Atelier Novikova in Ukraine from the designer's sketches. Tudor collars made for KEKA by Mason Sophy G.
- Materials named in brand copy: wool, linen, cotton, knitwear, organza.
- Owner requirements for the new site: full-screen video on open (desktop and phone); the collection browsable as an interactive wardrobe of garments on hangers; store and brand card in one.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Language: English only (owner decision; current site locale en_GB).
- Store status on the live site: "Online store / coming soon". Categories shown: Women, Men, Accessories, New In. The owner asked for a unisex presentation, so concepts browse by garment type, not by gender.
- Undecided: product list, prices, sizes, launch date, campaign film, production stack.

## Brand Commitments

- Name KEKA, domain keka.design, signature line "BE LIKE KEKA".
- Logo: serif "KE / KA" stacked inside a tilted square outline (files on the live site: keka_logo_dark.png, keka_logo_white.png).
- Voice: calm, precise, first-principles statements about form, identity and expression. No hype.
- Socials: instagram.com/keka_fashion, facebook.com/kekabrand.

## Evidence on Hand

- Runway photography of TRACE and Tudor collections and earlier lookbook images, downloaded from keka.design (Tudor photos credited to tomash.ph on the live site).
- Brand copy from the home and About pages, and four runway news articles.
- No product catalogue, prices, size charts, campaign video or press quotes beyond the news posts. Concepts use garment illustrations drawn from runway looks and sample prices, labelled as samples. Never present them as real stock or real prices.

## Product Principles

1. The garment is the hero; the interface recedes behind cut and fabric.
2. Brand and shop are one path: the brand card leads straight into the wardrobe.
3. Leave only what matters: one bold interaction per page, everything else quiet.
4. Works as well on a phone as on a large screen, video included.
5. Unisex by default: no gendered navigation.
