# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Concept stage: static HTML/CSS/JS prototypes, no build step. Production stack is undecided. The owner named Shopify's "hangers / interactive wardrobe" storefront trend as a reference, so a Shopify theme is the likely target, but that is not confirmed.

## Users

- Buyers of genderless, tailored clothing who already have their own point of view and buy pieces to keep, not trends. They arrive on phone and desktop, often from social media.
- Press, stockists and collaborators who use the site as the brand's business card.

## Product Purpose

keka.design is the KEKA brand site and its online store in one. It has to introduce the brand (who KEKA is, how it cuts) and sell the collection directly. Success: a first-time visitor understands the brand within one screen, browses the collection with pleasure, and can add a piece to the bag without hunting for the shop.

## Positioning

Unisex / genderless clothing built on tailoring, proportion and natural fabrics. Quiet luxury: restraint, construction and longevity over logos and seasonal noise. (Confirmed by the owner: unisex, quiet luxury and tailoring.)

## Operating Context

- The site opens on a full-screen video on desktop and on phone (owner requirement).
- The collection should be browsable as an interactive wardrobe: garments on hangers on a rail (owner requirement, after the Shopify trend).
- Store functions: product detail, size selection, bag.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Language: English only (owner decision).
- The current site keka.design could not be opened from the build environment (domain did not resolve), so its existing content, products, prices and assets were not reviewed.
- Undecided: product list, prices, sizes, fabrics, campaign footage, logo files, contacts, social handles.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: KEKA. Domain: keka.design.
- Character: quiet luxury, tailoring, genderless.

## Evidence on Hand

None in the repository. No product photography, campaign video, logo files, prices or press were supplied. Concept prototypes use synthetic garments, a generated placeholder video, and placeholder prices, all of which must be replaced with real material. Do not present them as real products or real prices.

## Product Principles

1. The garment is the hero; interface recedes behind cut and fabric.
2. Brand and shop are one path: the business card leads straight into the collection.
3. Restraint over noise: one bold interaction per page, everything else quiet.
4. Works as well on a phone as on a large screen, video included.
