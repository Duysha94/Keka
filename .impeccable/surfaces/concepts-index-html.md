Scope: KEKA home + store concept prototypes (concepts/index.html, hash views #conveyor, #backstage, #showroom). Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience and job: people who dress for identity, on phone and desktop; press and buyers using the site as the brand's card. Job: understand KEKA in one screen, browse the wardrobe, add a piece to the bag.
Pinned by owner: full-screen video on open (desktop and phone); interactive wardrobe of garments on hangers; English only; unisex browsing.
Material: real runway photography (TRACE, Tudor) and brand copy from keka.design; real logo. Garment illustrations and prices are samples, labelled.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The shop is a garment-care conveyor. KEKA pieces hang in covers on a looping rail; you call one to the counter by ticket number. Refuses the white product grid under a logo.
OWN-WORLD: Triplicate carbon tickets (pink customer copy, canary shop copy, blue garment tag), frosted covers, steel rail, wire hangers with printed shoulder guards, red rubber stamp, LED counter numerals, expanded signage grotesk.
STORY: Runway film passing like a conveyor, then "Fashion as identity. Form as freedom."; the visitor runs the rail, opens a piece, picks a size, stamps it into the bag.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed film of runway frames sliding past and stopping like a conveyor. KEKA logo top-left; Rail, About, Bag top-right. Bottom-left a pink claim ticket with the headline and "Go to the rail".
FORM: Dry-cleaner conveyor, candidate 7 of 7 (list re-derived after the live site opened), seed 817e671f.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Alternates built alongside (owner asked to see several concepts)

- Showroom (category standard, played straight): wooden hangers on a rail, product drawer, editorial about.
