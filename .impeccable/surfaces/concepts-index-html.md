Scope: KEKA home + store concept prototypes (concepts/index.html, hash views #conveyor, #pattern-room, #showroom). Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience and job: genderless-tailoring buyers on phone and desktop; press using the site as the brand's card. Job: understand KEKA in one screen, browse the wardrobe, add a piece to the bag.
Pinned by owner: full-screen video on open (desktop and phone); interactive wardrobe of garments on hangers; English only.
Constraints: no real products, prices or footage supplied. Garments, prices and film are synthetic and labelled.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The shop is a garment-care conveyor. Pieces hang in covers on a looping rail; you call one to the counter by ticket number. Refuses the white product grid under a logo.
OWN-WORLD: Triplicate carbon tickets (pink customer copy, canary shop copy, blue garment tag), frosted covers, steel rail, wire hangers with printed shoulder guards, red rubber stamp, LED counter numerals, expanded signage grotesk.
STORY: Film, then "Tailoring for any body"; the visitor runs the rail, opens a garment, picks a size, stamps it into the bag.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed looping film. KEKA signage top-left; Rail, About, Bag top-right. Bottom-left a pink claim ticket carrying the headline and "Go to the rail".
FORM: Dry-cleaner conveyor, candidate 7 of 7, seed 817e671f.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
